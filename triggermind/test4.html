// WHO WE ARE

More than a club. A space to make things happen.

TRIGGER MIND brings curious students together around technology, creativity and culture. Whether you're into coding, design, media, gaming, problem-solving or organizing events, there's room to contribute.

BUILD

Turn ideas into projects, prototypes and real experiences.

LEARN

Workshops, challenges and peer-to-peer learning without the boring stuff.

COMPETE

Take on quizzes, hackathons, challenges and creative competitions.

CONNECT

Meet people with different skills, interests and perspectives.
